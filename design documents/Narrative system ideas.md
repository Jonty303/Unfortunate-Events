# Unfortunate Events narrative system ideas

## Status

An exploratory narrative system, retained for future design work. The user explicitly asked to document the idea rather than implement it immediately. The currently accessible excerpt does not establish the full story, character identities, setting, or their earlier development.

## Two kinds of dialogue

Occasional large scripted story beats could pause or slow time for emotional exchanges. Alongside those, short event-triggered lines could express character during ordinary play.

The short lines should remain tiny, typically one line, and should not block gameplay. A cooldown would prevent chatter from overwhelming the player. Priorities could allow a near-death line to take precedence over a routine salvage line. Dialogue could show personality drift over time.

## Human and mind interaction

The recovered discussion proposed a human and a mind responding differently to the same event. One example used the destruction of salvage:

- Event line: “You just vaporized the salvage.”
- Human: “That was the pension fund.”
- Mind: “Resource denial, strategic.”

These are example lines from the discussion, not a finalized script. Salvage itself is not implemented in the prototype.

## Suggested first experiment

The assistant proposed about a dozen generic lines linked to low health, a large weapon being fired, and lost salvage, plus one slowed-time story beat after a tutorial. This was a proposed scope, not a confirmed implementation requirement.
